The bookmarklet

Install the Fill jury decision bookmarklet.

This bookmarklet pastes a sailing jury decision from your Google Doc into the right form, in one click. It automatically detects whether the page is sailti, RRS (racingrulesofsailing.org), or m2s (manage2sail) and fills the matching fields.

01

Drag the button to your bookmarks bar

Bookmarks bar hidden? Press Ctrl+Shift+B (Windows / Linux) or ⌘+Shift+B (macOS) to show it. On Safari: View → Show Favorites Bar.
02

How to use it

Once the bookmarklet sits on your bookmarks bar, you can use it on any sailti, RRS, or m2s decision form.

  1. In your Google Doc, click the red flag icon on the right edge to open the jury panel.
  2. In the panel, click Copy for sailti / RRS / m2s. The payload is automatically placed on your clipboard.
  3. Open the decision form in another tab — sailti, RRS, or m2s.
  4. Click the Fill jury decision bookmark. Fields populate automatically.

On RRS and m2s the bookmarklet switches to the Hearing tab automatically before filling. Validity checks, witnesses, scoring, and other system-specific fields stay manual — review them before submitting.

03

Updates

The bookmarklet loads the latest version of the script from this site every time you click it. There's no need to reinstall when fields or filling rules change.
